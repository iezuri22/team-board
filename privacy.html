Apply Yourself privacy policy

Apply Yourself is a private job search board used by Ifeanyi Ezurike and a small team helping with his job search. It is not a public service.

Gmail access

The board asks for one Google permission: to send email from the job search Gmail account that Ifeanyi connects. It uses it only to send the outreach emails he or his team schedule on the board. It does not read, store or delete any email in the account.

What is stored

The board stores the job applications the team logs, the contacts they add, the drafts they write and a record of what was sent. The Google sign-in is stored as a refresh token so scheduled emails can go out. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the board, or from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
